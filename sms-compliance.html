SMS program

Smart Hosting SMS Consent

How subscribers opt in

Subscribers navigate to the public Smart Hosting signup page and voluntarily provide a phone number. SMS consent is provided through a separate, optional, unchecked checkbox dedicated exclusively to text messages.

SMS use case

Operational/account notifications for hosts, co-hosts, property managers and authorized property staff, including reservation updates, guest access information, cleaning/turnover notifications and property automation alerts.

Required disclosure

By providing your phone number and checking the SMS consent box, you agree to receive SMS operational/account notifications from Smart Hosting. Message frequency may vary. Standard Message and Data Rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase. We will not share mobile information with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes.
