Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

1. Who we are

Smart Hosting is a technology and property-automation service operated by A&R Investment Venture LLC, based in Tucson, Arizona.

2. Information we collect

We may collect information you provide when you request information, create an account, configure a property, or communicate with us. This may include name, email address, phone number, property-related information, reservation information, and account information.

3. How we use information

We use information to provide, maintain, secure and improve Smart Hosting; support property automation; communicate with account holders and authorized property staff; process service requests; and comply with legal obligations.

4. SMS and mobile information

If you separately opt in to SMS communications, Smart Hosting may use your phone number and SMS consent status to send the operational/account notifications you requested, such as reservation, access, cleaning, service or property-automation notifications.

We will not sell or share your mobile information with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes.

SMS opt-in data and consent status are excluded from any categories of personal information that may otherwise be shared. We may share information, including SMS opt-in or consent status, with service providers that help us provide the requested messaging service, such as messaging platforms, carriers and technical vendors. Such sharing is for service delivery and not for third-party promotional or marketing purposes.

5. SMS consent is optional

Providing SMS consent is not a condition of purchasing or using Smart Hosting services. You may use available non-SMS communication methods where offered.

6. Data retention and security

We retain information as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements and meet legal requirements. We use reasonable administrative, technical and organizational measures to protect information.

7. Service providers

We may use third-party providers for hosting, communications, messaging, analytics, payment processing and other infrastructure needed to operate the service. Providers receive information only as reasonably necessary for their services and are expected to protect it appropriately.

8. Your choices

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information where applicable. You can opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. For SMS assistance, reply HELP or contact us using the information below.
